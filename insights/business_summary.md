# Business Summary

## Key Finding

While smoothies contribute relatively little revenue,
they exhibit one of the highest reorder rates.

This suggests that smoothie customers are highly loyal
and may represent a valuable customer segment.

## Recommendation

Rather than removing smoothies due to lower revenue,
SpeedySpoon should investigate what drives customer loyalty
within this category.

Potential opportunities include:

- Expanding the smoothie product line
- Creating targeted promotions
- Leveraging customer feedback
- Increasing cross-selling opportunities

## Business Impact

This analysis highlights the importance of evaluating
products not only by revenue, but also by customer
retention and repeat purchase behavior.
